One substantive refinement: natural sequence compatibility is an imperfect stability proxy. Extremely rare residues can have similar biochemical properties to observed alternatives; conserved CUE binding motifs can also reflect function. Blend exact residue ratios with exact biochemical-class frequency masses, cap extreme log-ratio tails, and add modest backbone/partition/volume/cap priors. Attenuate evidence when high gap fractions leave poor coverage. Raw fixed scores and frequencies remain unchanged and are retained in diagnostics. These choices were motivated by assay context, the raw alignment's remote-versus-close homolog composition, coverage and physical chemistry, not outcome-based model selection.
