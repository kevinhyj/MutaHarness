Model and audit

- There are no support measurements, target outcomes, or permitted MSA observations. Candidate repetition is not treated as homolog frequency. Scores are heuristic priorities, not calibrated growth or probabilities.
- The initial predictor (predict.py) produced checkpoint_predictions.json before refinement. It uses generic physicochemical changes, WT burial and torsion compatibility.
- One substantive refinement (refine.py) adds generic BLOSUM62 similarity, observed WT polar contacts/backbone donor contacts, and distinguishes exposed from buried proline/glycine changes. This corrects the baseline's coarse treatment of chemical-network loss and turn constraints. A weak functional prior is restricted to imidazole groups clustered in the actual coordinates, consistent with IGPD's metal-dependent function; these are not asserted to be annotated or experimentally verified metal sites.
- Small, capped contact adjustments permit local volume compensation and increased crowding only when WT sidechain contacts are observed. Removal of nearby proline weakens the positive-phi glycine-substitution penalty in a short turn. These are uncertain priors, not computed or validated epistasis.
- Risk combines accumulated damage (80%) and the largest individual disruption (20%). One monotonic tanh transformation is used for all 250 candidates, with higher scores predicting better growth. No block normalization, coefficient fitting, or model search was performed.

Raw CA coordinates were checked against the supplied mapped coordinates. The residue mapping is an exact whole-chain match. Every mutated residue has observed complete backbone and standard sidechain atoms. WT summary-tool recomputation matches supplied summary descriptors exactly.
- pLDDT is used only as a geometry reliability weight. RSA above 1 is preserved in source evidence; exposure is bounded only inside the heuristic weighting. Missing geometry would contribute no structural penalty, rather than being interpreted as zero exposure or zero fitness. Missing MSA is absent evidence, never a complete evolutionary score.
- No fixed partners are supplied. The single-chain AlphaFold model omits physiological IGPD oligomer interfaces, metals and bound substrates. The predictor does not change or infer partner sequences, mutant coordinates, relaxed energies, neutral fitness, or assay-specific epistasis.
- Execution validation established input identity, evidence consistency, score direction, finite bounds and exact ID coverage. It cannot establish predictive accuracy. No further scientific revisions were made in response to unmeasured ranking patterns.
