Final artifact: proposal.py, exposing propose(case, assignment).

Objective and assay interpretation
The target is positive signed rank correlation with GRB2_HUMAN_Faure_2021 yeast-growth DMS fitness. Negative fitness is legitimate. There is no observed wild-type control, so the model fits an unrestricted intercept and never inserts a zero-fitness wild-type observation. Output scores are ordinal values in [-1,1], not probabilities, calibrated assay predictions, or claims of improved wet-lab performance. selected_ids is always empty.

Measured feedback and refitting
All finite observed support outcomes in the supplied invocation are used. The supplied full case has 96 supports: 32 original singles plus 64 feedback records; one feedback record is itself a single, making 33 singles and 63 doubles. Sequence differences against the supplied reference identify substitutions. Each invocation rebuilds features, kernel matrices, training targets, validation weights and regression coefficients solely from its supplied supports. No stored support outcomes, fitted models, identifiers or prediction lists are read. The code is self-contained and requires numpy, scipy and threadpoolctl from the supplied scientific environment.

Scientific model
A regularized additive mutation kernel combines position effects, exact substituted residue effects, global physicochemical changes, and sharing of similar substitutions within coarse hypothesized SH3 structural categories. Scientific constants comprise amino-acid hydropathy, volume, charge, aromaticity, glycine/proline and polarity. The C-terminal SH3 core/pocket/glycine annotations are explicitly uncertain sequence-topology priors, with separate position effects permitting local deviations. Sharing uses a smooth RBF on differences of amino-acid descriptors. Unobserved substitutions and positions therefore inherit a conservative chemically informed estimate rather than a memorized fitness.

The main target transform is a normal-score rank transformation learned from training outcomes only. A smaller ensemble member uses an inverse sigmoid on the assay scale to represent global saturation and nonlinear combination effects. Its floor/ceiling are modeling settings, not established assay endpoints. Six nearby regularization/kernel/target configurations form an ordinal ensemble. Four-fold support-only feedback validation modestly updates prior ensemble weights; single-mutant-only subsets use ordinary support validation. All members then refit on all available support. Fixed seed 2601 and single-thread linear algebra give reproducibility. Query sequences supply only feature evaluations, never training labels or weight selection.

Validation and selection
Exploration compared raw-scale, rank-normal and inverse-sigmoid targets; additive position/substitution models; local chemistry kernels; wild-type sharing; sequence-distance smoothing; coarse structural sharing; and directly fitted nonlinear sigmoid models. Two repeated four-fold feedback holdouts retained singles in training and targeted signed Spearman correlation on doubles. Raw-scale models peaked near 0.49; transformed models without structural sharing peaked near 0.54; the best coarse-group rank model gave 0.603 and 0.660 in the two pooled repeated splits. These maxima are optimistic because configuration search reused the validation outcomes.

The final complete fitting procedure, including internal ensemble-weight fitting, was additionally run in outer four-fold feedback holdouts with seeds 2601, 731 and 1187. Pooled outer signed correlations were 0.578, 0.631 and 0.489 (mean 0.566). Outer targets were omitted entirely from fitting and target transformation. Two seeds overlap development partitions, so this is not a fully untouched external validation estimate. An original-single-information-only stress test ranked the observed double feedback at approximately 0.349; feedback supplies substantial useful information on combination behavior. Prediction was identical across repeated full-case calls, with every one of the 186 query IDs returned and no selections. Full fitting takes well below a second locally, leaving ample room under the 120-second execution limit.

Limitations
Only 96 outcomes support inference across many substitutions. Most exact combinations lack replication; arbitrary pair-specific epistasis cannot be identified reliably. Saturation is an empirical predictive assumption, not proof of folding, binding or growth mechanisms. Structural categories have no coordinates or experimental validation, and two queried positions lack direct observed substitutions. Sparse positions, distant chemistry and single-only training subsets remain uncertain. Internal validation cannot establish prospective wet-lab benefit or precise rank reliability for an individual query. No additional measurements, external data, pretrained scores, MSA, structures or hidden outcomes were used. Exploration artifacts are retained.
