Assay and objective
The target is signed rank correlation with the supplied GRB2_HUMAN_Faure_2021 yeast-growth DMS fitness. Higher observed fitness is better. Negative fitness values remain in their observed orientation. No measured wild-type outcome, zero-fitness baseline, probability calibration, or wet-lab improvement is assumed.

Final predictor
proposal.py is self-contained and deterministic. Every invocation estimates all model coefficients from the support records in that invocation. It never reads stored data/models/predictions and does not use IDs as predictive features. The reference and sequence differences define substitutions. All 96 current measured supports, including the 64 independently sampled feedback variants, enter the final fits. The 186 current queries supply sequence covariates only.

The final ordinal ensemble combines three fits by within-query rank averaging:
1. Weight 0.50: regularized kernel regression using mutation-position indicators, pooling among positions with the same reference amino acid, a mutation-count feature, and a degree-two kernel term. Kernel weights are position 4, reference-residue identity 8, count 1, intercept 1; quadratic weight 0.3/4; ridge penalty 0.1.
2. Weight 0.25: an additive kernel with position weight 4, reference-residue identity weight 2, count/intercept weight 1 and ridge penalty 2. This provides stronger shrinkage and an alternative combination assumption.
3. Weight 0.25: regularized latent substitution effects combined by a smooth minimum (temperature 0.05), with fitted intercept and count adjustment. Each latent substitution shares a position effect and weaker exact-substitution/chemical effects. Position weight 4, exact substitution 0.1, chemistry 0.25, ridge penalty 1. Chemistry uses ordinary hydropathy, volume, charge, aromaticity, polar groups, glycine, proline, cysteine and beta-branching constants. These are scientific descriptors, not pretrained fitness scores. The smooth minimum tests a worse-substitution-dominates hypothesis; it is not a demonstrated assay mechanism.

All ensemble weights and regularization settings are validation-selected configurations, not hardcoded learned mutation coefficients. The predictors fit the original fitness scale; output ranks are mapped into [-1,1]. selected_ids is always empty. No measurements are requested. NumPy seed is set to 2026; fitting itself is deterministic.

Validation and selection
Development used ten shuffled feedback holdouts (about 48 feedback records plus all 32 original supports for training; 16 feedback held out), and five mixed-support shuffled holdouts (72/24). Every fold was fitted from its training records only. Signed Spearman correlation was the selection criterion, with development feedback performance weighted 0.75 and mixed performance 0.25. Exploration compared exact-substitution and biochemical kernels, multiple regularization levels, quadratic interactions, monotone logit transforms, raw/ordinal targets, robust regression, smooth-min/additive pooling, and pairwise logistic ranking. The selected ensemble's development feedback mean was approximately 0.67 and mixed mean approximately 0.63; these are tuning results and are optimistic.

A fresh split-seed check used 25 additional feedback holdouts, 20 mixed-support holdouts, and eight half-support holdouts. The selected conservative ensemble averaged signed Spearman 0.6128 (feedback), 0.5131 (mixed), and 0.5334 (half-support); split standard deviations were about 0.1252, 0.1353 and 0.1150. These splits reuse the same 96 observations, are dependent, and are not an independent experimental test or a confidence interval. Fresh checks motivated using stronger shrinkage and only one pooled component rather than the more optimistic development blend.

Sequence-only SH3 segment and local-parity hypotheses were explicitly explored. They did not improve upon the selected residue-identity pooling and are not included in the predictor. Neither molecular contacts nor a fold mechanism were established. Pairwise ranking and stronger amino-acid sharing also did not warrant inclusion. Exploration scripts/results have been retained in the workspace; none is read by propose().

Limitations and verification
Ninety-six records provide sparse coverage of specific replacements and combinations. Position effects can dominate when a substitution is poorly observed. Quadratic terms and pooling express statistical assumptions, not proven physical epistasis. Fitness differences among nearly tied variants are particularly uncertain. Unseen positions transfer only through their reference amino-acid class; unseen substitutions have weak chemical priors in the pooled component. Internal correlations cannot establish generalization to all 186 unmeasured variants. Rankings carry no claim of experimental improvement.

Local checks verified exact query-ID coverage, finite bounded scores, empty selection, bit-for-bit repeated-call determinism, and changed fits when only half the supplied supports were retained. Three full/subset calls together took about 0.16 seconds in the local environment. The required protected executor check follows creation of this note and the final artifact.
