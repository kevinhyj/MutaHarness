The deliverable is proposal.py, a self-contained deterministic predictor using
only NumPy and SciPy. It fits from the support records passed to propose on every
invocation. There are no stored fitness values, coefficients, prediction IDs,
model files, external data, file reads, or cached fitted states. Query sequences
are used only to construct feature columns and ordinal scores. selected_ids is
always empty: no additional measurements are requested.

Assay interpretation
The supplied context identifies RASK_HUMAN_Weng_2022_abundance, with yeast growth
as the endpoint and ProteinGym DMS_score in original units. Higher observed
fitness is the signed ranking target. The model does not interpret these values
as calibrated abundance, probabilities, stability measurements, or evidence of
wet-lab improvement. No measured WT is supplied and no WT fitness is assumed.
The current 96 supports comprise 32 original single mutants and 64 independently
sampled feedback variants (59 doubles and 5 singles). All 96 outcomes inform the
final fit, rather than treating feedback merely as a selection history.

Model and use of feedback
Each support outcome is converted to its empirical midrank within the current
training set. This preserves assay direction and ties while reducing the
influence of extreme fitness magnitudes. Feedback allows recurring exact
substitution effects to be estimated in multiple backgrounds, supplies direct
information about double variants, and informs the mutation-count component.
The additive feature kernel combines exact position/substitution indicators,
position indicators, regional chemical descriptors, and mutation count/count
squared. Residue descriptors contain standard hydropathy, volume, charge,
aromatic/glycine/proline/polar/cysteine indicators, signed changes, absolute
changes, and products of original and substituted residue properties. Coarse
KRAS sequence/fold partitions supply region context; they are assumed sequence
annotations, never measured burial or structure coordinates. Their predictive
value was assessed using observed supports, not taken as established mechanism.

Training-only centering supplies an unpenalized intercept. Six kernel ridge
models use alpha in {0.03, 0.1, 0.3} and regional weight in {0.03, 0.1}, with
exact and position weights 1 and count weight 0.1. These are validation-selected
configuration constants, not stored learned effects. Every model is freshly
fit on every supplied subset. The models' query ranks are averaged and ranked
again to produce finite scores strictly inside [-1,1]. Absolute score values
carry no probability or assay-unit meaning. No random operations are used;
sorting records and vocabulary makes the computation reproducible under record
reordering, so no stochastic seed is required.

Scientific development and validation
An executable baseline was saved before development. Exploration compared
mutation/position pooling strengths, regional versus generic chemical effects,
substitution and amino-acid sharing, smooth sequence-neighborhood features,
coarse sequence-context hypotheses, and several regularization strengths.
Raw fitness, empirical ranks, Gaussianized ranks, asinh, signed square-root,
exponential target links and mixed raw/rank targets were compared. Polynomial
interaction kernels and regional RBF kernels tested nonlinear combination
assumptions. Pairwise logistic ranking, including smoothed pairwise targets,
was also evaluated. More complex nonlinear and pairwise approaches did not
consistently improve the selected rank-regression model.

The principal validation retains all 32 original supports and withholds 20 of
the 64 feedback supports, repeated over 20 deterministic search splits. Thirty
fresh splits with the same design provide a confirmation comparison. Fifteen
additional splits withhold 24 of all 96 supports to check mixed single/double
transfer. Outcomes and their ranks are computed only from each split's training
records; validation outcomes never enter its fit. The final six-member ensemble
has mean signed Spearman correlations 0.556 (search), 0.528 (fresh feedback
confirmation), and 0.593 (mixed holdouts). Exact-substitution/raw-fitness ridge
scores 0.402, 0.352, 0.375 under the same protocols. Exact-plus-position rank
ridge scores 0.493, 0.504, 0.462. These support the chosen regional model and rank
target but do not prove that its annotated regions correspond to physical
mechanisms. The confirmation splits are internal public-data checks, not a
blinded external evaluation; splits overlap and hyperparameter exploration
creates selection uncertainty. These figures must not be read as experimental
validation or guaranteed query performance. Exploration files are retained.

Limitations
There are only 96 measured variants, only 13 current queries have both exact
substitutions observed, and 42 have neither observed (131 have one observed).
Novel substitutions therefore require substantial shrinkage and extrapolation.
Position sharing cannot distinguish all amino-acid changes; coarse chemical
features cannot establish burial, contacts, folding free energies or biological
mechanisms. Pair-specific epistasis is insufficiently sampled and is not claimed
to be absent; rank fitting allows a flexible monotone assay-scale interpretation
but the final predictor remains additive in its latent feature representation.
The empirical support distribution, original-single sampling, and query
composition can differ. Uncertainty is greatest for unseen positions and rare
chemistries. Scores express a best supported ordering, not calibrated confidence.

Artifact checks
Local checks verified coverage of all current query IDs, finite bounded scores,
empty selected_ids, exact repeat determinism, invariance to record reordering,
and refitting after a smaller support invocation without surviving model state.
The predictor fit itself takes far less than the 120-second limit locally.
The required protected executor is run after this note is written; its output
is trial_output.json. Success of that check verifies artifact execution and
contracts, not experimentally established prediction quality.
