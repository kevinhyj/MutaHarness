Decision: support-only hierarchical kernel ensemble for ordinal KRAS abundance

Assay interpretation
The endpoint is the supplied RASK_HUMAN_Weng_2022_abundance yeast-growth DMS
score. Higher measured fitness is the signed ranking target. Negative values
are retained in their original units; no WT measurement, fitness=0 WT anchor,
probability calibration, or wet-lab improvement is assumed. The final output is
a monotone percentile ranking in [-1,1], with average ranks for exact ties.
selected_ids is always empty because no further measurements are permitted.

Model and feedback use
proposal.py is self-contained apart from NumPy/SciPy. On EVERY invocation it
extracts the supplied substitutions from reference/variant sequence differences,
constructs feature kernels, and solves new regularized support-only regressions.
All 96 current supports are used, including all 64 feedback variants. No support
outcomes, mutation-effect coefficients, IDs, prediction lists, fitted states or
training files are embedded or read. Query sequences only define features; they
do not affect the support/support kernel or the fitted support coefficients.
Assignment metadata does not filter query coverage.

The kernel shares information through exact substitutions, common sites, nearby
sequence positions, amino-acid properties (hydropathy, volume, charge, aromatic,
glycine, proline and polar indicators), substitution identity, and soft Ras
segment hypotheses. Intercept and mutation-count terms learn assay baseline and
mutation burden from the observations. Feedback gives repeated backgrounds
(e.g. multiple substitutions combined with a shared mutation), so background
effects are inferred jointly rather than estimated from an assumed WT value.
Quadratic positive-semidefinite kernel terms permit strongly regularized
combination effects. They do not assert additivity, physical contacts or specific
epistasis. A small sequence-environment term is retained in some ensemble members.

Ten kernel configurations fit original assay scores; the leading five also fit
monotone normal-rank transformed support outcomes. The two ensemble means receive
equal weight, followed by output ranking. This tests sensitivity to assay scale
while preserving signed direction. Kernel variance weights, distance scales and
ridge strengths in CONFIGS are validation-selected configurations, not learned
mutation coefficients. No randomized inference is performed. Development searches
used seeds 20261013 and 718; all validation splits and tree baselines were seeded.

Validation and selection
All development used only the 96 public measured support outcomes. Initial
selection used repeated four-fold feedback holdouts, retaining original supports,
plus repeated mixed-support holdouts (70/30 weighting of their mean Spearman
correlations). We tested exact/site baselines, physicochemical transfer, regional
and sequence-environment hypotheses, several positional distance scales,
quadratic combination terms, assay transformations, and ExtraTrees, random
forest and gradient-boosting baselines. Tree models and their tested blends were
inferior. Short-range positional transfer was more useful than extensive
sequence-environment hypotheses. The final ensemble spreads regularization and
feature assumptions rather than choosing one finely tuned member.

The actual final propose function was rerun on 16 feedback folds (seeds
101,211,307,409; 32 original supports plus 48 feedback records for each fit).
Mean signed Spearman: 0.620772; fold standard deviation: 0.131708.
On 12 mixed-support folds (seeds 113,223,317; 72 training records): mean 0.609783;
fold standard deviation: 0.093983. Fitting only the original 32 supports and
predicting the observed 64 feedback variants gave Spearman 0.531685. These are
different validation designs, so their difference is not a causal improvement
estimate. Logs and development files remain in the workspace.

A two-repeat outer feedback audit with model selection repeated inside the
outer training folds gave mean Spearman 0.553676 over eight folds, ranging
0.147059 to 0.750000. The candidate configurations were originally developed on
this same dataset, so even this audit is a sensitivity check rather than a fully
independent unbiased estimate. The final 0.62 validation figure was also involved
in ensemble selection. Repeated folds overlap and their standard deviations are
not confidence intervals. No internal result establishes experimental mechanisms
or predicts a guaranteed held-out correlation.

Limitations and delivery checks
Only 157 of 353 query mutation occurrences have exact substitution support.
Unseen effects require uncertain sharing by position and chemistry. There are
too few repeated combinations to identify detailed epistasis, and the data do
not establish which effects arise from folding, abundance, localization or
yeast-growth coupling. Ras segment and secondary-structure annotations are soft
expert hypotheses, not coordinates, MSA-derived conservation or measured burial.
No external sequences or pretrained predictors are used.

Local checks verified all 186 current query IDs, finite bounded scores, empty
selection, exact deterministic repeatability, fitting a changed public support
subset, and sensitivity to changed supplied support outcomes. A full invocation
took approximately 0.04 seconds locally, well within the 120-second fit limit.
The required protected executor is run after saving this note and proposal.py;
its contract output is trial_output.json.
