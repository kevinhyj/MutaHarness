The delivered predictor is proposal.py, exposing propose(case, assignment). Every invocation constructs all sequence features from the supplied reference and records and fits six regularized kernel regressions to ONLY that invocation's support fitness values. No stored training data, fitted state, prediction IDs, external sequence databases, pretrained scores, MSA, coordinates, or hidden outcomes are used. The saved exploration scripts and logs document development but are not imported or read by the predictor. All query IDs receive finite average-rank scores strictly within [-1,1]; selected_ids is empty. The model is deterministic, uses a fixed seed, and does not request experiments.

Assay interpretation: the context identifies YnzC stability measured by cDNA display proteolysis. Larger observed DMS_score is the signed ranking objective. We retain the observed numerical scale as a useful regression working scale, learn an intercept, and assume neither a measured wild-type score nor a wild-type value of zero. Returned scores are arbitrary ordinal ranks, not probabilities, calibrated stability values, confidence estimates, or demonstrated improvements in the laboratory.

The actual support contains 32 original single mutants and 64 independently sampled feedback variants: 17 additional singles and 47 doubles. The latter cover five position pairs. All 96 observations participate in the final fits with equal observation weight. The feedback learns pair backgrounds, substitution effects and departures from additivity that the initial single mutants cannot identify alone. Features include position indicators, changed-residue identities, site-specific chemical changes, global amino-acid changes, weak transfer across sequence positions, and regularized biochemical interaction terms. Standard hydrophobicity, side-chain volume, charge, relative helix propensity, and proline/glycine/aromatic/polar indicators are scientific constants, not learned assay values. Smooth amino-acid similarity kernels and Gaussian kernels over whole-variant chemical changes permit nonlinear combination effects. Each ensemble member uses a distinct regularization and smoothness configuration; averaging raw-scale predictions precedes bounded rank conversion. Kernel centering uses training records only. Query sequences are used only to evaluate their deterministic features.

A sequence-position basis includes a 3.6-residue periodicity as a possible alpha-helical prior. This is a predictive hypothesis supported only indirectly by the short, helix-compatible sequence, not a structure assignment. No specific salt bridge, contact map, burial assignment or mechanism is asserted. The chemical and nonlinear components have been evaluated predictively, not experimentally established as causal mechanisms.

Validation: explored additive hierarchical ridge kernels, chemical interaction kernels, smooth nonlinear kernels, raw/rank/asinh targets, ExtraTrees, random forests and gradient boosting. The initial search used three seeded repetitions of four-fold, mutation-order-stratified validation both across all support and with original single-mutant anchors retained while feedback was held out. Kernel hyperparameters were searched on these public support outcomes, then rounded to two significant figures. Six raw-target configurations formed the final ensemble; trees and transformed targets did not improve the chosen robust ensemble sufficiently to retain them.

A second set of five fresh split seeds checked the fixed rounded candidates and ensembles on (1) all-support four-fold splits, (2) feedback-only four-fold holdouts with original anchors retained, and (3) half-support training. Mean signed Spearman for the final ensemble was 0.860, 0.874 and 0.791 respectively. Corresponding within-double-mutant values were 0.694, 0.709 and 0.447; corresponding within-single-mutant values were 0.584, 0.545 and 0.479. These are means of complete out-of-fold ranking correlations over repetitions, rather than means of small fold correlations. They do not certify performance on the current unmeasured queries. The fresh splits reuse the same biological observations and also informed the ensemble choice, so this is an internal robustness check rather than an independent experimental test or fully nested unbiased performance estimate. Global correlations partly benefit from separating single and double mutants; the within-order figures expose the harder ranking task. Thin-support double ranking varied considerably across seeds.

Limitations: only 96 observations, uneven site/amino-acid coverage, few measured combinations, no external structure or evolutionary information, no replicate uncertainty, and potentially nonlinear/noisy proteolysis readout. Epistasis and physical effects are only identifiable where sampling permits; unseen sites and residue combinations require shrinkage and chemical transfer. Generic descriptors omit many sequence-specific contacts and assay artifacts. Validation uncertainty, hyperparameter search optimism and distribution shift remain. No further measurements are selected or requested. The final artifact's feature/kernel implementation was checked against the validation implementation; repeated full-case calls returned identical outputs and passed finite-score, complete-ID and empty-selection checks in substantially less than the 120-second fit limit.
