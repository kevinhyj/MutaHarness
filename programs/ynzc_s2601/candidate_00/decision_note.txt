The delivered proposal.py ranks the current unmeasured variants for the YnzC
cDNA-display proteolysis stability assay. Higher supplied fitness is better.
The numerical assay values are converted to empirical training ranks because
the objective is signed rank correlation. Neither zero fitness nor a measured
wild-type value is assumed. Returned scores are bounded ordinal ranks, not
probabilities, stability estimates in physical units, or evidence of improvement
in a future wet-lab experiment. No additional measurements are selected.

All 96 observed supports, including the 64 independently sampled feedback
variants, enter every full-data fit. In particular, the feedback provides direct
information about five recurring double-mutant position pairs and additional
single mutants. The model is rebuilt from the supplied support records on every
invocation: no outcome, variant ID, coefficient, fitted state or training dataset
is stored in the predictor. It can also fit a smaller supplied support subset.
Query sequences determine the feature layout and prediction inputs, but their
outcomes are never available or used.

The final model is an equal ordinal ensemble of four regularized kernel
regressions. Kernels share information through generic hydropathy, side-chain
volume, charge, helix propensity and residue classes; source-residue contexts;
site-specific mutation indicators, descriptors and amino-acid identities; and
pair-specific offsets and descriptor-product interactions. One member uses a
Gaussian similarity between amino-acid property vectors and a product kernel
for double-mutant combinations. This allows smooth nonlinear residue effects
and nonadditive combinations while regularization shrinks poorly supported
parameters. Intercepts and all fitted coefficients are estimated afresh using
only that invocation's supports. The fixed code stores generic constants and
validation-selected kernel amplitudes, not learned model coefficients.

Sequence-derived context features include distance from termini and the Gly20
region, candidate hydrophobic packing inferred from reference neighbors at
helical spacings, and sine/cosine helical phase. These are predictive hypotheses
based on sequence, not observed structure or experimentally established contact
maps. Charge interactions do not establish salt bridges, and learned statistical
nonadditivity does not prove a biochemical mechanism.

Development compared raw-scale, empirical-rank and normal-rank targets; additive
and interaction kernels; smooth amino-acid kernels; and ExtraTrees and gradient
boosting. Tree models were weaker, particularly for single mutants. Broad kernel
amplitudes were explored with seeded repeated stratified four-fold validation.
Metrics were Spearman correlations, assessed overall and separately within
single and double mutants to detect inflated performance from order separation.
Every validation fit ranked its own training outcomes, excluding held-out labels
from the target transformation. Rounded final amplitudes were subsequently
checked on eight additional split seeds, and on feedback-only validation with
the original 32 singles always retained for training.

For the four-member rank-voting ensemble, the additional ordinary support
validation gave mean correlations approximately 0.854 overall, 0.665 for doubles,
and 0.618 for singles (between-split SD approximately 0.020, 0.047 and 0.069).
Conditional feedback validation gave approximately 0.852 overall, 0.666 for
doubles and 0.305 for its 17 singles. These comparisons reuse a small public
support dataset during model development and are not an untouched external test
or a nested estimate of the entire feature/hyperparameter selection procedure.
They cannot establish accuracy on the hidden queries. Final models use more
training records than each validation fit, without claiming a quantified gain.

Limitations include sparse per-site observations, poorly identified higher-order
effects, lower confidence in unseen substitutions/sites, and substantial split
variability. Overall ranking is easier than fine ordering within mutation-order
groups. Proteolysis-specific sequence effects may coexist with folding stability
and are not separately identifiable here. No external sequences, MSA, structure,
pretrained predictions, hidden labels or measured WT were used. Prediction is
deterministic (no stochastic fitting); development seeds and all exploration
files are left in the workspace. The predictor performs no data-file reads.
