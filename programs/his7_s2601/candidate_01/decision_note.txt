Final predictor and objective
-----------------------------
proposal.py implements propose(case, assignment). It fits anew from the supplied
support records on every call, scores every current query ID, returns finite
ordinal midrank scores strictly inside [-1, 1], and always returns selected_ids=[];
no additional measurements are requested. It has no runtime training-data,
model-state, prediction-list, or external-resource reads. No IDs or observed
fitness values are embedded. Its only dependencies are the installed numpy,
scipy, sklearn and threadpoolctl packages. Computation is single-threaded; the
elastic-net solver is cyclic and carries an explicit reproducibility seed.

Assay interpretation
--------------------
The supplied assay is HIS7_YEAST_Pokusaeva_2019, yeast IGP dehydratase growth.
Higher original fitness means higher rank. There is no measured WT record and
we do not assign a measured WT fitness. The supplied 32 single-mutant supports
cluster around 1; the 64 independently sampled measured combinations range
from zero to above 1. Zero values are treated as tied observations, without
assuming their underlying molecular cause. Each fit transforms only its own
support fitness to empirical midranks. This matches the signed rank objective,
avoids assuming calibrated probabilities, and avoids imposing additivity in
the original growth units. The fitted baseline is the median single-mutant
support rank, or all-support median when single-mutant supports are absent.
It is a learned statistical baseline, not a claim about WT.

How measured feedback enters
----------------------------
All current support outcomes directly train every ensemble component. Mutation
indicators are extracted by comparison with the supplied reference. Features
also include mutation burden, a multi-mutant indicator, and eight assumed
position-pooling families with mutation-count terms. The families distinguish
visible co-mutated neighborhoods, including overlapping sequence intervals;
they are statistical annotations, not known structures or verified contacts.
A fallback group accommodates other positions. Sequence-only vocabulary may
include query substitutions; query records contribute no targets or fitted
coefficients. Novel substitution indicators have zero linear coefficients but
increase distance in the RBF model.

The final ensemble combines additive ridge (penalty 0.5), a second-order
polynomial kernel (linear + 0.2*linear^2/8, penalty 1), an RBF kernel
(exp(-0.03*squared_distance), penalty 0.005), and elastic net (alpha 0.004,
L1 ratio 0.2). Polynomial interactions permit nonlinear combination effects;
RBF interpolation permits smooth higher-order dependence. Regularization is
necessary because combinations are sparse. With 64 measured combinations the
four components receive equal weights. Below 64, the additive weight rises
continuously, capped at 0.55 at 32 or fewer combinations; the remaining models
share the residual weight. This modest count-based shrinkage preference follows
reduced-training validation. All coefficients and empirical target transforms
are recomputed on each invocation, including independent training subsets.

Validation and selection
------------------------
Development compared approximately 486 configurations: additive/contextual
ridge, explicit pair effects, stationary/polynomial kernels, elastic net,
random forests, extra trees, gradient boosting, and sequence-derived
hydrophobicity, volume, charge and residue-category features. Targets included
raw fitness, square-root fitness, empirical rank, and a regularized logit scale.
The signed Spearman statistic was used without taking its absolute value.
Main validation used three seeded repetitions (11, 47, 101) of four-fold
holdouts of measured combinations, retaining single-mutant supports in each
training fold. All target transformations were estimated within training folds.
The strongest component families were rank-based regularized regressions,
with mean pooled held-out correlation around 0.593. The equal four-model
ensemble produced correlations 0.590, 0.630 and 0.583 across the three repetitions
(mean 0.601). Tree models and generic physicochemical features did worse, so
they were excluded; explicit context copies also offered no consistent gain.

Sensitivity checks used 16 seeded random splits at each of 32 and 48 training
combinations plus the 32 single mutants. Equal-ensemble mean signed correlation
was approximately 0.429 and 0.588 respectively. The additive component was
slightly better at 32 training combinations (0.434), motivating the conservative
small-sample weighting rule. A raw-fitness single-support-only ridge comparator
was around -0.06 in these feedback holdouts, indicating that measured
combination data carry useful information for this task. This comparator does
not establish a causal effect of collecting feedback or wet-lab improvement.

These are descriptive internal validation results, not independent external
test performance. Configuration selection used these supports and can make
reported validation performance optimistic. Validation predictions, scripts,
and logs remain in the workspace for review; proposal.py imports none of them.

Remaining limitations
---------------------
There are only 64 measured combinations across several mutation neighborhoods.
Within-neighborhood validation varies markedly: pooled global correlation can
be stronger than fine ordering among closely related near-neutral variants.
Rare substitutions or interactions, including some fatal combinations, cannot
be identified reliably if no informative training examples remain. Unseen
substitutions receive a shrinkage/interpolation prediction, not a demonstrated
biophysical effect. Near-1 differences may include experimental noise. There
are no external sequences, pretrained scores, MSA, structures or hidden labels.
No proposed epistasis, contact, rescue mechanism, probability calibration, or
prospective wet-lab improvement is asserted. The output is only a ranking of
existing unmeasured variants under the available evidence.
