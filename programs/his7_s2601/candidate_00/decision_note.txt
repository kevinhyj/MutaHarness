The submitted predictor ranks the existing unmeasured variants for the supplied HIS7 growth assay. Higher measured ProteinGym DMS_score is better. It does not assume an experimentally measured wild-type value, infer probabilities of success, or claim wet-lab improvement. Final scores are ordinal ranks rescaled into [-1,1]; selected_ids is always empty.

Every invocation constructs features and fits all models exclusively from the support records passed through the case argument. It reads no training files, fitted states, external sequences or prediction lists. The final fit uses all 96 available measurements, including the 64 independently sampled feedback combinations. The actual query IDs are taken from the current case, never stored in the predictor. Seeds and model settings are fixed. The fitted model is not cached.

Sequence substitutions are encoded categorically. Mutation-library position blocks are explicit design covariates, inferred from the supplied library; they are not claims of physical domains or spatial contacts. Separate substitution features for combinations allow measured single-mutant effects to differ from effects on multiple-mutant backgrounds. Mutation count, its square, and combination/block intercepts capture shared background changes. Recurrent substitution pairs provide regularized nonlinear combination effects; a pair is included only if it co-occurs in at least two current support records, and never joins alternative amino acids at one position.

The final ensemble combines four ridge regressions of empirical support fitness ranks: a context model (alpha 1), and pair models with (alpha, pair-feature scale) equal to (1,1), (0.3,1), and (1,0.5). Their weights are 0.25, 0.20, 0.20, and 0.20. A depth-one gradient boosting model of original growth values receives weight 0.15. Model predictions are individually converted to query ranks before averaging, preventing incompatible target scales from dominating. The blend is again rank-normalized. Raw-growth boosting partitions offer complementary evidence, while regularization and averaging nearby settings control the variance of the small feedback dataset.

Development compared additive/context/pair ridge models, raw/rank/log/logit response scales, RBF and polynomial kernels, extra trees, random forests, gradient boosting, ordinal threshold logistic models, sparse regressions, and a penalized sigmoid global-epistasis model. Hydropathy, residue volume, charge, aromaticity, polarity, and proline/glycine features were tested as biochemical hypotheses. These richer models did not consistently improve the selected ensemble. No structural or causal mechanism is established by these comparisons.

The primary validation target was signed Spearman correlation on held-out feedback variants, retaining the 32 original singles as training observations. Development used three repeated four-fold partitions (seeds 17,43,81). After selecting the ensemble, five fresh repetitions used seeds 109,157,211,263,307 and rebuilt the entire vocabulary and recurrent-pair list inside each training fold. At 75% feedback training, the ensemble's mean correlation was 0.5918 with standard deviation 0.0372 across repetitions; context ridge alone was 0.5830. At 50% feedback training, the ensemble achieved 0.5675 (standard deviation 0.0520), versus 0.5608 for context ridge. Adding the biochemical comparator slightly lowered both mean correlations and was therefore omitted. These standard deviations describe split sensitivity, not confidence intervals or external replication. Model-search results were somewhat more optimistic than the fresh checks.

Remaining limitations: only 64 measured combinations cover several distinct mutation blocks; some substitutions have just one observation, and some query substitutions have none. Unseen substitutions therefore contribute only through known background/count features, rather than unsupported mutation-specific coefficients. Pair effects are associative, not causal, and higher-order epistasis may remain unresolved. Exact zero outcomes are treated as tied observations; close differences around neutral growth may be noisy. Validation is internal and cannot establish prospective experimental accuracy. Exploration scripts and their available results remain in the workspace.
