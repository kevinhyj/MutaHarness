The delivered predictor ranks GFP fluorescence, with higher signed scores meaning higher predicted assay fitness. It returns query-relative midranks in [-1,1], not calibrated probabilities, and always returns selected_ids=[] because there is no measurement budget. No measured wild type is assumed.

Every call fits anew using only the supplied support records. It never reads training files, prediction lists, fitted states, or external biological information. The 64 measured feedback variants enter the same supervised fits as the original single mutants. Exact mutation, residue position, amino acid substitution chemistry, mutation count, and aggregated physicochemical changes provide complementary information. Four deterministic tree regressors model nonlinear combinations: two ExtraTrees models with different leaf sizes, a chemistry-only random forest, and an ExtraTrees model with additional substitution and sequence-derived features. A strongly regularized ridge model uses a coarse bright/dim auxiliary target; the 2.8 separator is an assay-scale configuration selected in development. The raw regressors retain continuous measured fitness. Predictions are combined as equally weighted ordinal ranks.

Single-mutant fitness values are summarized by residue position on every invocation and provide a small additive correction to the ensemble. The reference level for this correction is estimated from the currently supplied single mutants, not hardcoded and not a WT measurement. Position transfer is deliberately only a correction: different substitutions at one residue can have different outcomes.

Validation used only observed supports, never query labels. Development compared raw-fitness, normal-score rank, transformed latent-fluorescence, and binary ridge models; additive and polynomial features; fractional logistic and sigmoid regression; random forests, ExtraTrees, and histogram gradient boosting. Three repetitions of four-fold feedback holdout retained original supports in training, reflecting the experimental feedback setting. Mutation-count-only Spearman correlation on the feedback was 0.597. Initial tree/ridge ensembles obtained approximately 0.66, and positional singleton anchoring reached approximately 0.68 on those development folds. These numbers are subject to model-selection optimism. Additional fold seeds and ranks computed separately within each validation query batch gave a mean Spearman correlation of 0.636 for the final architecture and correction weight, versus 0.626 without the correction. Two repetitions of four-fold mixed original/feedback holdout gave 0.625, versus 0.613 without correction. These are internal estimates, not independent wet-lab confirmation; original measurements remain available in feedback-only holdout. Conservative equal model weights and a small correction were retained rather than aggressively optimizing the ensemble.

The measured assay has a dense dim floor near 1.3 and a brighter population. Nonlinear regressors can represent saturation and threshold-like combination effects without asserting a mechanism. A coarse auxiliary classifier helps distinguish populations, while rank averaging avoids mixing incompatible numerical scales. No claim of experimentally improved variants follows from this ranking. Fine ordering near the floor has weak evidence, and within-bright rankings are substantially harder than distinguishing bright from dim.

Scientific constants include conventional hydrophobicity, volume, and charge descriptors. Approximate secondary-structure ranges, chromophore-position flags, and alternating reference-sequence hydrophobicity are assumed sequence annotations and feature hypotheses; no coordinates or MSA were available. The alternating hydrophobicity proxy is not an experimentally demonstrated burial map. Their usefulness was evaluated alongside chemistry-only models, and the delivered ensemble distributes weight across multiple representations. Strongly nonlinear or substitution-specific epistasis cannot be reliably identified from 96 observations. Unseen sites/substitutions and unusually high mutation burdens require extrapolation.

All estimators use seed 14 and one computation worker. No fitted coefficients, measured support outcomes, support IDs, or query IDs are embedded in the predictor. proposal.py contains all inference code. extra_features.py is retained as a development helper; inference does not import it. Development scripts, feature arrays, and validation outputs are retained for inspection but are not read by inference. Contract checks verify deterministic repeat fits, every current query ID, finite bounded scores, and an empty selection.
