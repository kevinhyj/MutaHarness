Purpose and available evidence
This proposal ranks the current fluorescence/FACS variants of Chlamydomonas
reinhardtii phototropin (PHOT_CHLRE_Chen_2023). Higher observed fitness is better.
Only the supplied 96 support outcomes and reference/variant sequences were used.
No external sequences, pretrained predictions, structures, MSA, hidden labels,
previous evaluation outputs, or additional measurements were used or requested.

How the measured feedback is used
The 64 measured combinations identify substitution main effects and departures
from additivity within the recurring combinatorial mutation library. The 32
original single mutants mostly cover a different set of substitutions. They
provide a robust single-mutant baseline and same-position estimates for the four
single-mutant queries. Each invocation reconstructs sequence differences and
fits entirely afresh from its own supplied support records. There are no saved
coefficients, fitness values, variant IDs, prediction lists, or fitted states in
the executable. The exploratory files are retained for reproducibility but are
never read by proposal.py. The assignment is not used to request experiments:
selected_ids is always empty, and every supplied query ID receives a score.

Model and assay interpretation
The combination model averages 64 kernel-ridge configurations. Every model has
shared substitution main effects and an independently fitted, unpenalized
intercept. Pairwise effects have stronger regularization. The interaction
families are all pairs involving residue 46, all pairs involving either 46 or
60, and a small-prior ensemble of all distinct-position pairs. Fixed prior
masses are 0.45, 0.45, and 0.10 respectively. Ratios and regularization strengths
are documented in proposal.py. Same-position alternate substitutions are never
allowed to form an interaction. Interactions involving positions 46 and 60 are
validation-selected statistical hypotheses, not structural contact assertions.

Both original assay units and a square-root transformation are considered.
No measured WT is assumed and no additive effects are computed by subtracting
an invented WT fitness. Transformations are modeling choices for this positive
readout; they do not establish a fluorescence mechanism. Models are averaged
on an assay-like scale after undoing the square-root transformation. On EVERY
invocation with enough combination supports, two seeded four-fold support-only
CV repetitions estimate signed Spearman correlation for each configuration.
Smooth prior-weighted exponential weights (temperature 0.035) retain uncertainty
rather than hard-selecting a winner. Small-data fallbacks are deterministic.
Final values are average ranks mapped into [-1,1], without interpreting them as
probabilities, calibrated assay values, or experimental improvement.

Scientific validation
Development compared raw, logarithmic, fractional-power, and squared targets;
additive models; general pairwise kernels; kernels focused on different loci;
RBF kernels; sparse elastic-net and Bayesian ARD fits; direct pairwise logistic
ranking losses; gradient boosting; and mutation-burden interactions. Tests used
multiple independently seeded splits of the observed feedback records, including
training on only 32 combinations. Broad sparse selection and tree models were
less reliable here. Focused regularized interactions and averaging of assay
scales were retained. The initial executable additive baseline is preserved as
proposal_baseline.py. Exploration scripts and their local results are retained.

After fixing the complete predictor, final validation used four new repetitions
of four-fold feedback holdout, with ensemble CV nested inside each training
fold. The original single supports were retained, and no held-out feedback
outcomes entered fitting or inner CV. With 48 training combinations, mean test
Spearman rho was 0.85423 (fold SD 0.07364), versus 0.79357 for the early additive
baseline. Eight new 32-training/32-test feedback splits gave mean rho 0.75559
(SD 0.03035), versus 0.64878 for that baseline. These are descriptive internal
estimates, not independent wet-lab evidence. Broad configuration searches and
interaction-family selection used this small public dataset, so residual model
selection optimism remains even though the final split seeds were new.

Artifact checks and limitations
The full supplied case fit took about 0.11 seconds in the local single-thread
scientific environment, far below 120 seconds. Contract checks found 186 finite
scores within [-1,1], exact query-ID coverage, and an empty selected_ids list.
Repeated invocations and reordered supports produced identical outputs.
The protected executor is the required final integration check.

There are only 64 combination outcomes, no replicate uncertainty estimates,
and no direct evidence for most pairwise or higher-order mechanisms. Assay
transformations and locus-focused interactions remain predictive assumptions.
Single-mutant queries outside measured positions receive a conservative median
baseline rather than unsupported detailed structural predictions. Same-position
single estimates are shrunk toward that baseline; their extrapolation remains
uncertain. Validation primarily evaluates combinations because they constitute
182 of the 186 current queries. Generalization to new mutation alphabets,
unseen combinatorial regimes, or unrelated assay contexts is not established.
